"Eric Harris", "Dylan Klebold" are names that will never be seperated--remaining together forever. On a quiet, peaceful day...students at Columbine High School were gearing up to head to lunch. Little do they know, 2 fellow classmates--Eric and Dylan--are about to pull out 4 guns, and start their deadly massacre. Killing 12 students and 1 teacher. Really, the kill count does not include AFTER the massacre. The Columbine Effect, people taking their own lives in despair...It's truly disturbing how much this brutal event affected the lives of many.



Eric David Harris, born on April 9th, 1981 in Wichita, Kansas. His father, Wayne Harris, worked in the Air Force as a transport pilot. He had 6 different bases located in New York, Ohio, and Michigan. Wayne retired in 1993 due to cutbacks and went back to Colorado. 2 years after, Eric started to attend Columbine High School. Columbine seemed to affect Eric in ways which caused him MANY problems. Eric was an avid DOOM fan. He played it constantly, also making WADs himself in which he let Dylan be a beta tester. So... what was the reason? Well, there's many reasons and factors in which why he did what he did.



Dylan Bennet Klebold, born on September 11th, 1981 in Lakewood, Colorado. Dylan was considered a gifted kid, being in the CHIPS program. Dylan and Eric met in their middle school, Ken Caryl. Columbine has affected both Eric and Dylan in prominent ways. Klebold had a journal in which he wrote down his suicidal feelings. It also contained his homicidal thoughts, and when he first mentioned up a massacre. Dylan mostly yearned for his 'halcyon', or a girlfriend. So... what was the reason? Again, many factors. He was depressed, just wanting to die. In his journal... He seemingly wrote a 'suicide note'. He had expressed these thoughts since ~'97.
